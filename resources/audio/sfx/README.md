# Super Mario Bros. (NES) World 1-1 audio cues

These WAV files were downloaded unchanged from [The Mushroom Kingdom's **Super Mario Bros. (NES)** sound archive](https://themushroomkingdom.net/media/smb/wav). Each file's source URL is `https://themushroomkingdom.net/sounds/wav/smb/` followed by its filename. The files are 16-bit, 22,050 Hz, mono PCM. `SHA256SUMS` records the downloaded bytes.

The set covers cues that can occur during World 1-1: 1-Up, brick smash, bump, coin, fireball, fireworks, flagpole slide, small and Super Mario jumps, kick, death, pause, pipe travel/power-down, power-up pickup and appearance, stomp, and the low-time warning. The game-over and stage-clear jingles are included for the level's possible outcomes. Bowser and vine cues, which do not occur in World 1-1, are omitted.

The [original NES game disassembly](https://6502disassembly.com/nes-smb/SuperMarioBros.html) identifies the game's sound effect cues, including distinct small and big jumps. These WAVs are archival recordings of the NES version, not ROM audio data or a cycle-accurate NES audio emulator. Exact hardware output depends on the NES audio circuitry and concurrent music; a WAV recording cannot establish sample-for-sample identity with every original console playback.

The game currently plays the brick smash, small and Super Mario jumps, coin, power-up appearance, and power-up pickup clips. The other downloaded cues are available for future gameplay events.
